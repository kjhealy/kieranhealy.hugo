In anticipation of the second edition of [_Data Visualization_](https://socviz.co), which is coming later this year from Princeton University Press, version 2.0.0 of my `socviz` package is now on [CRAN](https://cran.r-project.org). The update removes some functions that aren't needed anymore and adds a couple of sample datasets.
